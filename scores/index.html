Questions

Is this sheet music really free?

Yes. Every PDF here is free to download and print, with no sign-up and no email required.

What’s the difference between the piano solo and the chord edition?

Both have every note. The chord edition adds chord symbols above the staff, so you can simplify the left hand, read the harmony at a glance, or improvise your own version.

How hard are these pieces?

Each piece lists its own level. Tapestry of Whispers, Obsession and Halcyon are early intermediate, roughly RCM 3–4 or ABRSM 2–3. Luminescent Dreams is intermediate, roughly RCM 5–6 or ABRSM 4–5. Grade equivalents are approximate.

I like Ludovico Einaudi. Will I like these?

If you enjoy Einaudi, Yiruma or Joep Beving, these sit in a similar neo-classical world: songlike melodies, repeating patterns you can learn by sections, and harmony that rewards slow practice.

Why does Obsession have an A minor edition?

The recording is in G minor. The A minor chord edition is the same piece transposed, for players who would rather read it without flats.

Can I hear a piece before I learn it?

Yes. Every released title links to its recording on Spotify, and the album is also on Apple Music.

Yes. Free for personal use, teaching and performance. Please don't resell or redistribute.
