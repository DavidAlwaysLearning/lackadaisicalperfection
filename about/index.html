About David Rivera

American composer

David Rivera is an American composer of piano-led instrumental music, with touches of electronica and accompaniment. He writes every piece and plays and samples every instrument on his recordings himself.

His catalog spans eight albums, from Melodic Subtlety in 2006 to Lackadaisical Perfection, due in January 2027. Free scores for several pieces are on the Scores page.

David is a member of ASCAP.
