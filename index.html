Lackadaisical Perfection

coming soon · solo piano

Eleven unpublished pieces. Anxious tranquility, a voyage, a tapestry of whispers, an undersea odyssey. The titles hold two feelings at once. The title track sits inside the set, not out in front.

Nothing to stream yet. The room is open so you can see what’s coming.

I’m David Rivera. Florida neoclassical composer. Seven piano solo and ambient albums that fuse minimalism and electronica. The music is piano at heart. Subtle melodies for overlooked moments. A place to slow down and take the time to listen.

Lackadaisical Perfection is my next album. I’m finishing the sound files now. Be on the lookout.

When I’m not composing, I sail.

The pieces

  1. 1Anxious TranquilityThe title says it all. Driven and steady. Close your eyes and this piece will transport you; twice, from a slow E minor dream into a D minor scape. The piano starts at 84 bpm, lifts to 97, and then a real ritard into the last chord. Continuous arpeggios, restless, even. Tranquility perhaps in the last seconds.
  2. 2Epic VoyageThe voyage is epic, not tranquil, maybe a bit anxious, as all adventures are. It's a C minor piece traveling at 130 bpm.
  3. 3Intentionally ForlornThere may be times when you crave to be intentionally forlorn. The piano takes you there. And yet, a wandering cloud of aleatoric notes tempts you with somewhere else. C# minor at 110 bpm.
  4. 4Mellowitude in ThreeWhen alone you may crave a quiet mellowitude in three. Playful, simple, clean. Clear thoughts, reminiscing solitude. C major. The tempo moves ever so slightly between 173 and 181.
  5. 5Fugacious EternityEternity is gone in an instant. Let this piano and an orchestra of ethereal instruments accompany you for a moment. D minor, 110.
  6. 6Drops of HesitationEach note hesitates, like a drop wondering where it will fall. And so, timid drop by timid drop, we fill the river of life. G minor, wavering tempo.
  7. 7Absurd ExpectationThis beautiful and simple piano melody is small and perfect. It is an absurd expectation. F# minor at 145.
  8. 8Lackadaisical PerfectionA peaceful, easy feeling — when a melody is allowed to just be. F major at 90.
  9. 9Fortuitous DiscoveryA piano song is found by a quiet stroke of fortune. And so, all harmonizes.
  10. 10Tapestry of WhispersDelicate notes are whispers that form our thoughts. Music weaves who we become.
  11. 11Undersea OdysseyA slow gloomy waltz, now an undersea odyssey. Delicate organic instruments with synthesizer textures, unfolding like a cinematic story through melancholy, a little hope, and introspection.
